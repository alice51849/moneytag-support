Expense tags that add up across projects

Categories tell you where money went inside one budget. But the question is often wider: how much did I spend on travel, software or materials across everything I'm running? In MoneyTag, each project keeps its own books, and tags add up across all of them, so you get both answers from the same entries.

Who this is for

  • Freelancers and side hustlers who keep each client or gig as a separate project but want one total for things like “mileage” or “software”
  • Anyone running a renovation, an event or a trip as its own budget who still wants to see one kind of spending everywhere
  • People who want income and expenses on the same tag, so the tag shows a net figure, not only a spending total

How tags work in MoneyTag

  • Projects are separate ledgers. Each project has its own transactions, its own currency and its own bottom line: income minus expenses is its net result.
  • Tags go on any income or expense. Tags are a first-class part of MoneyTag, not an afterthought.
  • The Tags screen totals a tag across all projects at once — income, expenses and the net for that tag. Cross-project tag analysis is part of Lifetime Pro.
  • One currency per project. Choose the input currency on the Home screen and use public rates refreshed about every 6 hours or a manual rate; the amount is saved in the project's base currency.
  • The same ledger on iPhone and iPad, an Apple Watch app for adding a transaction from your wrist, and Home and Lock Screen widgets that show the current project's income, expenses and net.

Set it up in five steps

  1. Create a project for each client, gig or budget and pick its base currency.
  2. Decide on a short list of tags you will reuse everywhere (for example “travel”, “software”, “materials”).
  3. Each time you add an income or expense, give it one of those tags.
  4. Check each project's net result to see how that project is doing.
  5. Open the Tags screen to see a tag's income, expenses and net across all projects; export to CSV or PDF when you need to share or file it (Lifetime Pro).

FAQ

Where do I see the total for one tag?

Open the Tags screen. It totals the same tag across all of your projects at once and shows the income, the expenses and the net for that tag. Cross-project tag analysis is part of Lifetime Pro.

Can a tag be used on income as well as expenses?

Yes. You can tag any income or expense, so a tag can show money coming in and money going out, plus the net.

Do tags replace projects?

No. A project is a self-contained ledger with its own transactions, its own currency and its own net result. Tags cut across projects, so you can see one kind of spending everywhere it happens.

What happens with expenses in another currency?

Each project has its own base currency. On the Home screen you choose the input currency and use public rates refreshed about every 6 hours or a manual rate; the converted amount is saved in the project's base currency.

What can I do without buying anything?

You can start free with one project and a limited number of entries. Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited projects, tags and reports, plus CSV, PDF and photo export and backup and restore.
