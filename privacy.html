Where your ledger lives

Projects, transactions, amounts, notes, tags, categories and settings stay in MoneyTag’s on-device storage and are not sent to Frankfurter / ExchangeRate-API. With iCloud Sync on, one copy also lives in your own iCloud.

Exchange rates

Each project has its own base currency. On the Home screen, choose the input currency directly. MoneyTag uses public exchange rates refreshed about every 6 hours, or you can set a manual rate and later reset to automatic. The converted amount is saved in the project’s base currency, and saved rates remain available offline. When automatic rates are on, MoneyTag sends the three-letter base-currency code and the necessary network request to api.frankfurter.dev / open.er-api.com. Frankfurter / ExchangeRate-API’s Cloudflare infrastructure may process the IP address, approximate region, interaction data and diagnostics. This processing is for app functionality and analytics, may be linked to you, is not used for tracking, and involves no advertising. Turn off automatic rates and use a manual rate to avoid these requests. Reference rates are provided by Frankfurter or ExchangeRate-API; for informational use.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details; the app only learns from Apple whether the purchase exists, and records the unlock locally on your device. Restoring a purchase asks Apple, not us.

Export, backup and sharing

Export and backup files are created on your device and go wherever you send them through the iOS share sheet. Once a file leaves MoneyTag it is handled by whichever app or service you chose, under that provider's own privacy policy.
