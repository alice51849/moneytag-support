Privacy Policy

Everything stays on your device. No account, no tracking. Rates use a public exchange-rate service.

Last updated 2026-08-14

Everything stays on this device. MoneyTag’s only non-Apple network use is fetching public exchange-rate quotes: open.er-api.com is the primary service and api.frankfurter.app is the fallback. No transactions, projects, tags, settings or personal data are sent. Apple separately handles in-app purchases and purchase restoration. Neither service requires an API key. It contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party code that would talk to a server.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the MoneyTag app for iPhone and iPad, together with its Apple Watch app and its widgets.

Data we collect: none

MoneyTag has no sign-up, no login and no user account. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifier or any device identifier. We receive no usage statistics, no crash reports and no analytics events, because the app never sends any.

Where your ledger lives

Everything you enter — projects, transactions, amounts, categories, tags, notes and settings — is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. The Apple Watch app and the widgets read that same data through Apple's on-device app group and Watch Connectivity, which stay entirely within your own devices.

Exchange rates

MoneyTag’s only non-Apple network use is fetching public exchange-rate quotes: open.er-api.com is the primary service and api.frankfurter.app is the fallback. No transactions, projects, tags, settings or personal data are sent. Apple separately handles in-app purchases and purchase restoration. Neither service requires an API key. Each project has its own base currency. On the Home screen, choose the input currency directly. MoneyTag uses public exchange rates refreshed about every 6 hours, or you can set a manual rate and later reset to automatic. The converted amount is saved in the project’s base currency, and saved rates remain available offline. It contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party code that would talk to a server.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details; the app only learns from Apple whether the purchase exists, and records the unlock locally on your device. Restoring a purchase asks Apple, not us.

Export, backup and sharing

Export and backup files are created on your device and go wherever you send them through the iOS share sheet. Once a file leaves MoneyTag it is handled by whichever app or service you chose, under that provider's own privacy policy.

Children

MoneyTag is a general-purpose tool with no data collection, no advertising, no social features and no content shared with other people, so nothing personal can be gathered from a child who uses it.

Your control and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is no account to close and no data of yours for us to hand over or erase. You stay in full control: deleting a transaction, a project or the app itself removes that data from your device permanently. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page is updated and the date above changes with it. We will not start collecting data quietly — any such change would appear here and in the App Store privacy details first.
