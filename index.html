Frequently asked questions

1What exactly is a project?
A project is a self-contained ledger: its own transactions, its own currency and its own bottom line. MoneyTag adds up the income, subtracts the expenses and shows the net result for that project alone — so a side business, a home renovation and a family trip never get mixed together.
2Does MoneyTag convert between currencies?
No. Each project uses one currency that you pick when you create it, and MoneyTag never applies an exchange rate. Every figure you see is exactly the numbers you typed, added up. If you need a second currency, create a second project for it.
3How do tags work?
Tags are a first-class part of MoneyTag, not an afterthought. Tag any income or expense, and the Tags screen totals that same tag across all of your projects at once — income, expenses and the net for the tag. Cross-project tag analysis is part of Lifetime Pro.
4What is free, and what does Lifetime Pro add?
The free version gives you one project and 30 transactions, with no time limit and no ads. Lifetime Pro is a single one-time purchase — never a subscription — and unlocks unlimited projects and transactions, cross-project tag analysis, custom categories and tags, CSV and text export, and backup and restore.
5I bought Lifetime Pro, but a new device shows it locked.
Open Settings in MoneyTag and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. MoneyTag has no account of its own — Apple keeps the record of the purchase, and restoring is free and unlimited.
6Where is my data stored? Is there a cloud backup?
Everything stays on your device, in MoneyTag's own storage. There is no account, no server and no cloud sync — which also means nobody but you can recover your ledger. With Lifetime Pro you can create a backup file and keep it yourself. Deleting the app deletes its data with it.
7What do the Apple Watch app and the widgets show?
The Watch app lets you add a transaction from your wrist and see the current project's income, expenses and net. The widgets show that same status on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen. Both read data your iPhone already holds, through Apple's on-device sharing — nothing travels over the internet.
8Does MoneyTag need an internet connection or any permissions?
No. The app makes no network requests at all and works in Airplane Mode. It asks for no location, contacts, photos or notification permissions. The only moment anything leaves your device is when Apple processes a purchase or a restore.
9How do I export my ledger or move it to another device?
With Lifetime Pro, Settings offers export (CSV or plain text, through the iOS share sheet) plus a full backup file and restore on the other device. Because there is no sync, that backup file is how you move or keep your data.
