Frequently asked questions

1What exactly is a project?
A project is a self-contained ledger: its own transactions, its own currency and its own bottom line. MoneyTag adds up the income, subtracts the expenses and shows the net result for that project alone.
2Does MoneyTag convert between currencies?
Each project has its own base currency. On the Home screen, choose the input currency directly. MoneyTag uses public exchange rates refreshed about every 6 hours, or you can set a manual rate and later reset to automatic. The converted amount is saved in the project’s base currency, and saved rates remain available offline.
3How do tags work?
Tags are a first-class part of MoneyTag, not an afterthought. Tag any income or expense, and the Tags screen totals that same tag across all of your projects at once — income, expenses and the net for the tag. Cross-project tag analysis is part of Lifetime Pro.
4What is free, and what does Lifetime Pro add?
Free · 1 project · 5 entries left. Unlimited projects, tags and reports — one purchase, yours forever. Export CSV · Export PDF · Backup & restore.
5I bought Lifetime Pro, but a new device shows it locked.
Open Settings in MoneyTag and tap Restore purchase while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. MoneyTag has no account of its own — Apple keeps the record of the purchase, and restoring is free and unlimited.
6Where is my data stored? Is there a cloud backup?
Everything you enter — projects, transactions, amounts, categories, tags, notes and settings — is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. MoneyTag’s only non-Apple network use is fetching public exchange-rate quotes: open.er-api.com is the primary service and api.frankfurter.app is the fallback. No transactions, projects, tags, settings or personal data are sent. Apple separately handles in-app purchases and purchase restoration. Neither service requires an API key.
7What do the Apple Watch app and the widgets show?
The Watch app lets you add a transaction from your wrist and see the current project's income, expenses and net. The widgets show that same status on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen. Both read data your iPhone already holds, through Apple's on-device sharing — nothing travels over the internet.
8Does MoneyTag need an internet connection or any permissions?
MoneyTag’s only non-Apple network use is fetching public exchange-rate quotes: open.er-api.com is the primary service and api.frankfurter.app is the fallback. No transactions, projects, tags, settings or personal data are sent. Apple separately handles in-app purchases and purchase restoration. Neither service requires an API key. It asks for no location, contacts, photos or notification permissions.
9How do I export my ledger or move it to another device?
Export CSV · Export PDF · Backup & restore. Includes projects, transactions, tags and settings.
